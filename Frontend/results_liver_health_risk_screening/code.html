What You Can Do Next

Structured preventive actions tailored to moderate risk stratification:

Discuss results with your doctor

Bring this printed summary to your general practitioner or gastroenterologist for an ultrasound or FibroScan.

Maintain a liver-healthy dietary pattern

Emphasize Mediterranean-style eating, whole grains, and lean proteins while lowering saturated fats.
