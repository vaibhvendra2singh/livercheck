shield

Why screening matters

Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH / NAFLD) is often called a silent progression. Over 80% of individuals with early stage liver fibrosis experience zero symptoms.

Non-invasive serum scoring formulas (like FIB-4 and APRI) allow primary care doctors to rule out advanced scarring with 90%+ negative predictive value before irreversible liver damage occurs.

Sample Lab Report Guide

How to extract relevant values from your blood panel

Transaminases are listed under your Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) or Liver (Hepatic) Function Tests. Platelet counts are listed in your Complete Blood Count (CBC).

TEST NAME IN RANGE REFERENCE RANGE UNITS
ALT (SGPT) 42 7 - 56 U/L
AST (SGOT) 38 10 - 40 U/L
ALBUMIN 4.4 3.5 - 5.0 g/dL
PLATELET COUNT (CBC) 215 150 - 400 x10E3/uL
TOTAL BILIRUBIN 0.7 0.2 - 1.2 mg/dL

Note: In some diagnostic laboratories, ALT may appear labeled as "SGPT" and AST may appear as "SGOT". These indicate identical biochemical markers.
