01 / Biological Parameters

What it measures

Triangulating transaminases, hematology, and systemic metabolic state.

vital_signs

Alanine transaminase (ALT), Aspartate transaminase (AST), and Gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT). Detects ongoing parenchymal hepatocyte turnover and bile duct strain.

Key Biomarkers ALT · AST · GGT
water_drop

Functional Capacity & Platelets

Circulating thrombocyte counts adjusted for biological chronological age. Platelet depletion correlates closely with splenic pooling and portal hypertension onset.

Body mass index (BMI), waist-to-height ratio, and fasting glycemia or HbA1c. Quantifies visceral lipotoxicity, peripheral insulin resistance, and MASLD propensity.

Key Biomarkers BMI · Waist · FPG
info

The Diagnostic Blind Spot of Standard Liver Panels

Routine annual liver panels (ALT and AST alone) frequently produce false-negative reassurance. Clinical studies show that up to 40% of individuals with biopsy-proven early-to-moderate fibrosis (F1–F2) maintain strictly normal transaminases. Without integrating visceral adiposity indices and platelet-to-enzyme non-linear dynamics, early parenchymal architecture loss remains undetected until symptomatic portal congestion manifests.

02 / Algorithmic Architecture

How the model works

LiverCheck applies an externally validated clinical regression model calibrated on cross-sectional epidemiological liver biopsy and transient elastography (FibroScan) cohorts.

By synthesizing enzyme ratios (AST/ALT), platelet counts, and visceral adiposity indicators, the model calculates the probability of significant liver scarring (Ishak stage ≥3 or Metavir ≥F2).

Step 01 / Inputs
Biochemical Triad
Transaminases, Platelet count, Fasting Glucose input via client-side runtime.
Step 02 / Weighting
Log-Odds Ratio
Calculates De Ritis ratio (AST/ALT) weighted against age-decay platelet curves.
Step 03 / Correction
Metabolic Matrix
Applies non-linear penalties for visceral adiposity and suspected hyperinsulinemia.
Step 04 / Stratification
Tri-Tier Output
Maps calibrated probability into rule-in, indeterminate, or rule-out zones.

05 / Clinical Constraints

Limitations & Boundary Conditions

LiverCheck is explicitly constructed for population screening stratification. It is subject to known mathematical and biological constraints:

arrows_outward

Not a Substitute for Elastography or Biopsy

FibroScan transient elastography, magnetic resonance elastography (MRE), and histological tissue biopsy remain gold standards. This tool informs when to seek these tests.

warning

Acute Liver Injury Distortion

Temporary viral hepatitis flares, drug-induced liver injuries (DILI), or acute alcohol episodes spike transaminases drastically, invalidating chronic fibrosis calculations.

group

Age Boundaries (20 to 79 Years)

Mathematical regression weights are uncalibrated for pediatric cohorts or patients ≥80 years, where physiological platelet depletion can falsely exaggerate risk scores.

fitness_center

Pregnancy & Strenuous Muscular Exertion

Gestational hemodilution alters baseline platelet concentration. Intensive resistance exercise causes rhabdomyolysis-related AST release, distorting liver specificity.
